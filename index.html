Why we build here

The nutrition aisle serves allergen-free households by substitution rather than by design. Products built around what has been removed taste like compromise, and the people who need them most are the ones already reading every line of every panel. We formulate for the constraint first and let the nutrition and the eating experience follow from it.

How we work
Ingredient-led formulation
We select for amino acid completeness, fibre quality and sensory performance, and we print the ingredient count on the front of the pack.
Dedicated allergen-free manufacturing
We manufacture only in facilities that handle none of the fourteen major allergens.
Short lists
If an ingredient does not do a specific job, it is not in the formula.
